How to play

  • Aim at balloons. Whatever your crosshair is on takes damage automatically, at your fire rate, up to your range. Hold LMB to fire twice as fast.
  • Chain pops for a combo. Every pop within 2.4 s of the last one raises your multiplier. It pays for itself fast.
  • Walk up to a kiosk and press E. Eight of them ring the plaza. Shift+E buys as many levels as you can afford.
  • Golden balloons drift up and away. Pop one before it escapes for a cash bonus and 20 s of triple income.
  • Bloatoons show up every few hundred pops. Big, slow, extremely profitable.
  • Rebirth (R) wipes your cash and upgrades for permanent prestige multipliers — and unlocks new arenas.
  • The pedestal in the middle is the arena portal. Stand on it and press E to travel; later arenas pay far more.
  • Your progress saves automatically, and you keep earning a share of your income while away.
